# Lab 5- Create Visual Content Using the Create Experience in Microsoft 365 Copilot

## Lab Objective

In this lab, you will learn how to use the **Create experience in
Microsoft 365 Copilot** to quickly generate **executive-grade visual
content**---without design skills.

You will create visuals that executives typically need for:

- Leadership communications

- Company-wide updates

- Board-level messaging

- Internal change initiatives

By the end of this lab, you will be able to:

- Access the Create module

- Generate images, banners, and videos using natural language

- Customize visuals using templates

- Reuse content across leadership communications

## Lab Scenario

You are a **senior business leader** (CXO / VP / Director).

This month, you are responsible for:

- Communicating a **strategic update** to employees

- Announcing a **new initiative or change**

- Sharing progress with leadership and teams

- Ensuring messages look **professional, clear, and credible**

You **do not have time** to:

- Open design tools

- Brief designers

- Iterate on layouts

You will use **Copilot Create** to produce **ready-to-use executive
visuals** in minutes.

## Prerequisites

- Microsoft 365 Copilot subscription

- Access to the Microsoft 365 Copilot app

- Modern browser and internet connection

**Note**: Some Create features may vary for Personal or Family plans

## Exercise 3: Build an Internal Leadership Newsletter Banner

**Executive Scenario:**

As a CHRO or COO, you send a monthly leadership update to all employees.

**Steps**:

1.  In the **Design a banner** create window, paste the following prompt
    and click **Create** to generate the banner.

    ```
    *Create a banner for an internal leadership newsletter.*

    *Use a modern, minimal design with a white background.*

    *Title: "Leadership Update -- This Month"*

    *Font should feel professional and executive-friendly.
    ```

    ![](media/media/imaged.png)

2.  Review the banners generated for monthly updates newsletters:

    ![](media/media/imagee.png)

**Note**: Multiple banners will be generated based on the provided
prompt. Review the options and select the one you prefer.

### Expected Outcome

- A clean, modern banner appears that can be reused in internal
  newsletters.

## Exercise 4: Create a Change or Initiative Campaign Image

**Executive Scenario:**

As a **CIO or Transformation Lead**, you are rolling out a **new
operating model or policy**.

**Steps:**

1.  Paste the following prompt and click **Generate** the image.

    ```
    *Create a professional image to support an internal change
    initiative announcement.*

    *Use confident, modern visuals and include text: Driving Change.
    Delivering Impact.
    ```

    ![](media/media/imagef.png)

2.  Review the promotional campaign image generated for **Driving Change, Delivering Impact Image.**

    ![](media/media/image10.png)

***Note**: User can edit, copy, download image as needed from the
**Transform with AI** toolbar from the right-hand side of the **Create
an Image** window*

### Expected Outcome

- A **change-management visual**

- Suitable for email headers, intranet posts, or presentations

## Exercise 5: Create a Short Executive Update Video

**Executive Scenario:**

As an **Executive Sponsor**, you want to share a **short video update**
instead of a long email.

**Steps:**

1.  In the prompt box, paste the following prompt:

    ```
    *Create a short executive update video.*

    *Use a clean, modern style with light background music.*

    *Include on-screen text:*

    *"Executive Update: Key Priorities This Month.*
    ```

    ![](media/media/image11.png)

2.  Review the promotional short mp4 video generated.

3.  Click on the link to export the video. The video will be downloaded
    to your computer in mp4 format.

4.  You can also download the executive update video pacakage that
    contains the video as well as music. Click on the link to download
    the video.
    
    ![](media/media/image12.png)

### Expected Outcome

- A **short, professional executive video**

- Ideal for leadership visibility and engagement
